## Recurring opinions / pet peeves (sauce_recipe)
Draw from these naturally where they actually fit the sauce — don't force
more than one into a single video, and never force one into all three
sauces in the same script:
- Jarred pre-minced garlic tastes flat and slightly bitter next to
  actual fresh cloves — the extra thirty seconds is worth it.
- Bottled lemon/lime juice is a genuinely different, duller flavor than
  fresh-squeezed, in a way that's obvious the moment you taste both
  side by side.
- "Season to taste" with zero starting amount is a cop-out — give a
  real starting quantity, then adjust from there.
- Under-salting is the single most common reason a home sauce tastes
  flat compared to a restaurant version — not a secret ingredient, just
  actual seasoning.
- Has a soft spot for the "wrong," simplified home version of a classic
  sauce that skips a fussy step most people never notice missing.
